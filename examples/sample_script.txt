DUST ON THE GLASS

FADE IN:

EXT. SOLAR FARM - DAY

MARA stands between rows of panels. She picks up the radio.

MARA
Block four is brown. We go now or we lose the afternoon.

JULES (O.S.)
Copy. Drone two is at twelve percent.

She checks the tablet. A dust wall sits on the horizon.

EXT. SOLAR FARM - NIGHT

Same rows. MARA is still here, no time cut stated. She holds the radio.

MARA
We should not be here.

INT. CONTROL SHED - NIGHT

JULES sits at a bank of monitors. He grabs the thermos.

JULES
Say again. I cannot see you on the feed.

A hologram of the farm flickers, then dies.

EXT. ACCESS ROAD - NIGHT

ELENA waits by a truck. She never appears again.

ELENA
If the yield drops under forty, they pull the permit.

FADE OUT.
